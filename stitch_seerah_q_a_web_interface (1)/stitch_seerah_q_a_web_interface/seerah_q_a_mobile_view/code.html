What were the key events during the Early Meccan Period before the public call?

Early Meccan Period

The Early Meccan Period, prior to the public call, is generally characterized by a few significant, private developments over approximately three years.

Following the initial revelation at Hira, the Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) began sharing his message privately, focusing on his closest family and friends. This phase is often referred to as the 'secret call' (Sirriyyah).

  • The First Believers: Khadijah (his wife), Ali ibn Abi Talib (his cousin), Zayd ibn Harithah (his freedman), and Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (his close friend) were among the very first to accept Islam.
  • Dar al-Arqam: As the small group of followers grew slightly, they needed a safe place to gather, learn, and pray. They met secretly in the house of Al-Arqam ibn Abi'l-Arqam, located near Mount Safa.

During this time, the focus was on establishing the foundational beliefs of monotheism (Tawhid) and strengthening the spiritual resolve of the early Muslims, before facing the wider Quraysh community.
